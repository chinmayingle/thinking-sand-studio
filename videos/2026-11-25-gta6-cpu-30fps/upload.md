**Title:** GTA 6 Is 30 FPS Even on PS5 Pro. The GPU Isn't the Problem

**Description:**
The PS5 Pro's GPU is up to 45% faster than the PS5's (Sony's claim), yet GTA 6 runs at 30 FPS on both. Rockstar says the Pro's CPU is "not powerful enough to run the game's complex simulation at higher frame rates."

Here's why: every frame, the CPU has to move the whole city (cars, people, physics) before the GPU can draw it. At 30 FPS that work gets 33.3 ms per frame; 60 FPS would leave only 16.7 ms. And the PS5 Pro kept the same 8 Zen 2 CPU cores as the PS5, a design that first shipped in 2019, clocked only about 10% higher (up to 3.85 GHz vs 3.5 GHz). A faster GPU can't draw a frame the CPU hasn't finished, so it waits, and the Pro spends that headroom on looks instead: sharper upscaling (PSSR) and ray-traced shadows. Digital Foundry measured a near-flawless 30 FPS on PS5 Pro, with the worst dip at 24 FPS in an explosion stress test.
