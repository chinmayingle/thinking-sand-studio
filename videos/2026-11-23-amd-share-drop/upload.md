**Title:** AMD Tops the Gaming Charts. It Still Lost 5 Points of Desktop Share
**Alternates:**

**Description:**
AMD's X3D chips top most gaming charts, yet AMD's share of desktop CPUs shipped fell from 34.9% to 29.9% in three months, while Intel went from 65.1% to 70.1%. How?

Market share doesn't count benchmarks; it counts chips shipped. Every office PC, school PC and gaming rig is one chip, one vote. And the whole pie shrank: desktop shipments fell more than 20%. Mercury Research's working theory is that weak laptop sales left Intel with spare chips it moved into desktops, while AMD leans more on PC gamers, who built fewer new PCs with graphics cards and RAM this expensive. So AMD may not have lost on speed at all.
