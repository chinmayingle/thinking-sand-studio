**Description:**
32 GB of DDR5-6000 once sold for $72. By late October 2026, the cheapest kit Tom's Hardware tracks cost $447, about 6x more. In Germany, a 2x48 GB DDR5-6400 kit went from about €304 to €2,779 (9.1x), and 3DCenter's DDR5 price index sits at 607% of its July 2025 level.

Why? Your PC's RAM and AI's memory are cut from the same silicon wafers, and memory makers are moving capacity to server memory for AI. Worse, AI's memory (HBM) needs roughly 3x the wafer area of DDR5 for the same capacity, according to Micron. So every AI server soaks up memory your PC would have used. TrendForce expects prices to keep rising, just more slowly. This Black Friday, the best deal is the RAM you already own.
