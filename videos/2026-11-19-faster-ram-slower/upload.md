# Upload text: "Faster RAM made games slower" (Short, 57.9 s)

**Title:** 33% Faster RAM, 8% Fewer FPS: DDR5-8000 vs 6000
**Alternates:**
- Faster RAM Made This Game Slower. Here's Why
- DDR5-8000 Lost to DDR5-6000 (The Middleman Problem)

**Description:**
DDR5-8000 is 33% faster than DDR5-6000 on the box. In Horizon Forbidden West, it was 8% slower. Why?

Your CPU never talks to RAM directly. Every request goes through the memory controller. At DDR5-6000 it keeps pace with the RAM: 3,000 MHz, tick for tick. At DDR5-8000 it can't keep up, so it drops to half speed, 2,000 MHz. Faster RAM, slower middleman. Games need lots of tiny answers fast, so every slow round trip leaves your CPU waiting.
For gaming on Ryzen, reviewers recommend low-latency DDR5-6000. On a big-cache X3D chip, slow vs fast RAM made only about a 2% difference.
